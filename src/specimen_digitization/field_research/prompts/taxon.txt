Field: Taxon (taxon). The scientific name the labels give the specimen, at the rank written.

Your source: lookup with source "gbif". GBIF matches the name against the pinned Catalogue of Life checklist and decides (G23); Catalogue of Life and Global Names Verifier answer alongside as support. A supporting source that disagrees is worth a sentence in the explanation; it never overrides GBIF.
- Query with the whole scientific name exactly as a reading prints it: a capitalised genus, then lower-case epithets, a subspecies or variety with its marker as written, with the author and year when written ("<Genus> <epithet>", "<Genus> <epithet> <subspecies>", "<Genus> <Author>, <year>"). GBIF's answer for part of the name settles nothing. Never send place words, collectors, dates or a name no reading prints.
- G25: "sp.", "sp. 1", "cf.", "aff." or "nr." right after the genus makes a genus-level identification. Look up the genus alone; never ask for a species. "<Genus> sp. 1" is looked up as "<Genus>".
- A genus, or a word that may be a genus, written behind a qualifier of any spelling ("cf.", "cfr.", "c.f.", "aff.", "nr.", "conf.", "poss.", "prob.") or with "?" ("<Genus>?", "?<Genus>") is in doubt, on whatever label it stands.
- Still look up a doubtful genus alone: the bare genus word, with no qualifier and no epithet ("<Genus>" for "cf. <Genus> <epithet>"). That lookup settles nothing: then answer sources_cannot_resolve, whatever GBIF says.
- A morphocode with no genus ("sp. <n>") names no scientific taxon: never invent a genus for it.
- Before you answer for a morphocode, look up alone any genus that any label writes anywhere: on another line or another label, with a qualifier or without. Only a genus a reading of these labels writes counts: never look up a genus no reading writes. Only when no label writes a genus does the morphocode stand alone: then answer sources_cannot_resolve and quote it.
- A common name or other text that is not a scientific name is not a taxon.
- When readers spell the name differently, look up each variant. If GBIF confirms exactly one and finds nothing for the others, that one settles; its reading gives the literal.
- Never reduce a species to its genus to get an easier match.
- A candidate whose literal is part of a longer name its quote writes ("<Genus> <epithet>" quoting "<Genus> <epithet> <subspecies>") cannot settle the taxon: answer several_possibilities or sources_cannot_resolve.

Resolve only on a GBIF answer with status success for the name your literal writes (or its genus, under G25), and cite its evidence_id. Its first candidate is GBIF's decision: value and authority_id are that candidate's, copied exactly; another candidate is never the value.
- G28: an exact synonym clears with GBIF's accepted name. The literal stays the label's spelling; value is the accepted name copied exactly from GBIF's candidate, with its authority_id.
- When GBIF's matched name differs from the literal (an authorship added, the genus of a genus-level name), copy the candidate's name into value and its authority_id.
- Homonyms, fuzzy or variant matches, a match at a higher rank than written, or a usage that is not accepted are not resolved: answer several_possibilities with the candidate names, or sources_cannot_resolve.
- GBIF finds no match for every variant you tried: sources_cannot_resolve, naming the variants.
