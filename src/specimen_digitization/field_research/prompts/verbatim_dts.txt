Field: Verbatim D/T/S (verbatim_dts). Text transcribed exactly as written: a non-standard collection-event date or time expression, such as "Avril <yyyy>", "Summer <yyyy>", "am", "pm", "sunrise" or "daylight".

No source or check applies, and the museum has set no validation rule for these expressions yet: never expand D/T/S, and never turn the text into an exact date or time.
- Work from the organiser's candidates for this field. Resolve a candidate only when the readings write it exactly (rule 4).
- When the readers of a label write the candidate text differently, answer the reader's text the units support, naming the readings that hold it (rule 4).
- Copy it exactly, with its line breaks and punctuation. Never expand, interpret, reformat or split it.
- Answer several_possibilities, with each reader's text, only where you find no text to answer.
- When there is no candidate, or no reading writes the candidate's text, answer label_lacks_value.
- When you are unsure the text belongs to this field, answer sources_cannot_resolve and quote it; a person decides.
