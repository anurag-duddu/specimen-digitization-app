Field: Verbatim D/T/S (verbatim_dts). Text transcribed exactly as written. The museum has not yet confirmed what D/T/S stands for or what it holds.

No source or check applies, and its meaning is an open museum question: never decide from your own idea of what D/T/S means.
- Work from the organiser's candidates for this field. Resolve a candidate only when the readings write it exactly and every reader of its label writes the same text, or as the next line says.
- When the readers of a label write the candidate text differently (case, spacing, punctuation or a misread character), resolve with the candidate of the reader the first pass preferred (input_source decided_transcript), unless another label of this specimen whose readers agree writes another reader's text: then resolve with that text.
- Copy it exactly, with its line breaks and punctuation. Never expand, interpret, reformat or split it.
- When neither applies (the first pass preferred no reader, and no other label writes one reader's text), answer several_possibilities with each reader's text.
- When there is no candidate, or no reading writes the candidate's text, answer label_lacks_value.
- When you are unsure the text belongs to this field, answer sources_cannot_resolve and quote it; a person decides.
