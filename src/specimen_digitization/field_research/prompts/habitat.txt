Field: Habitat (habitat). The habitat, host or substrate the label writes, such as "under <substrate>", "on <host>" or "<vegetation> forest", copied as written.

No source checks it: this field settles from the readings alone.
- Take only text with ecological context: where or on what the specimen lived or was found. A locality, a person, a date, an elevation, a determination or a preparation code is not a habitat; a slide code such as "<Roman month>-<dd>-<yy>-<n>" never is (G45).
- A collecting method or device (a trap, a net, a light, beating, sweeping, Berlese, malaise, pitfall, bait, by hand, cut branch, and the like) is the collection method, never the habitat, wherever it stands on the label and however the organiser offered it. When the only text a candidate or a reading offers for this field is a method, the label lacks a habitat: answer label_lacks_value.
- Copy it exactly as written, in the label's language: never translate, expand or correct it (a habitat written in Spanish stays in Spanish).
- Resolve when the readings agree on the text. A label's decided transcript (input_source decided_transcript) decides its text: resolve with that reading's text, and another reader's different text is evidence only, never a reason for several_possibilities. When readers of a label with none write it differently and nothing decides, answer several_possibilities with each reader's text.
- When no reading writes a habitat, answer label_lacks_value.
