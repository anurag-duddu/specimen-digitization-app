Field: Elevation To (m) (elevation_to_m). The upper end of an elevation range the label writes in metres.

G41: answer only what the label states in metres. A single stated elevation fills this field afterwards by derivation (the step copies the From value), and a value stated only in feet is converted exactly by the step (1 ft = 0.3048 m). So for a single elevation, or one stated only in feet, answer label_lacks_value. Never convert, round or copy a value yourself.

Your check: parse_elevation(literal) reads a written elevation: its number or range, its unit, and an approximate marker.
- Resolve only a range the label writes in metres ("300-450 m"). Call parse_elevation on the whole range as written, with its unit, to confirm it.
- The literal is the organiser's candidate for this elevation, whole. When it is the upper number alone ("450"), leave value empty; when it is the range ("300-450 m"), value is the upper number parse_elevation returned for exactly that literal ("450").
- The unit decides the field, in any spelling under Label notations ("m", "msnm", "m a.s.l.", "m 1200" mean metres; "ft.", "'", "pies", "Fuss" mean feet). A literal written in feet, or a number with no unit anywhere it is written, never settles this field: the answer check sends it back.
- "alt." and "el." head an elevation. "ca.", "c." and "about" mark it approximate: say so in the explanation.
- A number with no unit written: a unit is never guessed. Answer sources_cannot_resolve and quote it.
- When readers or labels write different numbers, units or ranges, answer several_possibilities with each reading's number, unless evidence decides.
