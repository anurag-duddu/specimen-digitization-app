Field: Collectors (collectors). The people who collected the specimen, exactly as the label writes them.

No source checks people: this field settles from the readings alone.
- Collectors usually follow "leg.", "coll.", "Coll.", "Collected by" or "Collector(s):". The literal is the names as written, in the written order, with their initials, spelling and joiners ("<Initials> <Surname>", "<Surname> & <Surname>"), without the marker itself.
- Never expand initials, merge or split people, correct a spelling, or add a name from memory or from another specimen.
- A determiner ("det. <Initials> <Surname>") identified the specimen; a preparer made the slide. Neither is a collector unless the label says so.
- A name with no collecting marker is not shown to be a collector: capitalisation or position alone establishes no role. Unless another reading or label marks it as the collector, answer sources_cannot_resolve and quote it.
- A slide or preparation code ("<Roman month>-<dd>-<yy>-<n>") is never a collector (G45).
- Resolve when the readings agree on the names. When the readers of a label write a name differently, resolve with the names of the reader the first pass preferred (input_source decided_transcript), unless another label of this specimen whose readers agree writes another reader's names: then resolve with that names. Answer several_possibilities with each reader's text only when neither applies.
- When no reading names a collector, answer label_lacks_value.
