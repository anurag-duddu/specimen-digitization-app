Field: Collection Method (collection_method). How the specimen was collected, as the label writes it, such as "Berlese", "light trap", "beating" or "sweeping".

No source checks it: this field settles from the readings alone.
- Take only text with collecting context: a trap, a technique or an apparatus. A locality, a person, a date, an elevation, a determination or a preparation code is not a method; a slide code such as VI-24-68-7 never is (G45).
- Where the specimen lived or was found ("under bark", "on fungus") is the habitat, not the method.
- Copy it exactly as written, in the label's language: never translate, expand or correct it ("Berl." stays "Berl.").
- Resolve when the readings agree on the text. A label's decided transcript (input_source decided_transcript) decides its text: resolve with that reading's text, and another reader's different text is evidence only, never a reason for several_possibilities. When readers of a label with none write it differently and nothing decides, answer several_possibilities with each reader's text.
- When no reading writes a collection method, answer label_lacks_value.
