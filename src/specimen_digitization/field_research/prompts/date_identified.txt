Field: Date Identified (date_identified). The date the specimen was identified (determined), at the precision written.

Your check: parse_date(literal) returns every reading the date's notation allows under the profile's date rules, each as an ISO date at its precision (YYYY-MM-DD for a day, YYYY-MM for a month, YYYY for a year).
- The identification date is written with the determination: after "det." and the determiner's name, or on a determination label ("det. <Initials> <Surname> <year>"). A collecting date is never the identification date, even when it is the only date on the labels.
- Slide or preparation codes ("<Roman month>-<dd>-<yy>-<n>", "<mm>-<dd>-<yy>-<n><letter>") and any part of them are never dates.
- Call parse_date on the exact literal. Resolve with value set to one ISO reading it returned; never write a date the check did not return. A year alone is a valid date at year precision.
- parse_date reads Roman months in any position ("<dd>.IX.<yyyy>", "<yyyy>.IX.<dd>"), month names and abbreviations in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian and Latin, and ordinals ("3rd", "1er"). A date split over two lines of one label, or a day and month with their year on one line ("det. <Initials> <Surname>, <Roman month>-<dd> <yyyy>"), is read as one date only under the rules parse_date applies (via split_lines, one_line, year_on_next_line, year_on_previous_line; a year with a measurement marker attached, or with other text on its line, is not taken). This field settles only from a determination's date: a date that a determination stands before on its line, with no collector or complete date between them ("det. <Initials> <Surname> <yyyy>", "det. <dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy>", "det. <Initials> <Surname>, <Roman month>-<dd> <yyyy>"), or a date alone on its line under a determination ("det. <Initials> <Surname>" above "<dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy>", "Det. by <Initials> <Surname>" above "<Month> <d>, <yyyy>"); a determination claims the first complete date after it only ("det. <Initials> <Surname>" above "<yyyy>" above "<dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy>": the year alone on its line is its date). A collection mark after the date is no collector ("det. <Initials> <Surname> <yyyy> ex coll. <Surname>", "det. <Initials> <Surname> <dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy> Coll. <ACRONYM>", "det. <Initials> <Surname>" above "<dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy> Mus. coll. <Surname>" are identification dates); a surname after coll. may begin with initials or lower-case particles ("coll. van <Surname>"). When the word after coll. or col. may be a surname or a collection (a word of capitals with a vowel, "coll. <SURNAME>"; a lower-case word; initials with no name after them), and the date is the determination's only if it names a collection, no date field settles (coll_may_name_a_collector_or_a_collection): never resolve it. Any other date is refused here (date_follows_no_determination): one a determination follows ("<dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy> leg. et det. <Initials> <Surname>"), one with a collector after it on its line ("det. <Initials> <Surname>" above "<dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy> leg. <Surname>" or "<dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy> coll. <Initials> <Surname>"), one whose line holds other text ("det. <Initials> <Surname>" above "<Place> <dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy>"), or one after "Ident." and a number ("Ident. No. <n>, <Place>, <dd>.<Roman month>.<yyyy>"). A year passed as year_literal never settles a value: the step checks the literal again without it. Readings of the label that give different results for the literal leave it ambiguous (readers_disagree_on_date): never resolve it.
- G24: a two-digit year is 19xx only under the profile's century rule, which parse_date applies. G29: a numeric date gives both month-day and day-month readings unless a part over 12 fixes which is which, and parse_date calls it ambiguous (day_month_order_ambiguous): never resolve it to one; resolve on a literal parse_date settles (a named or Roman month), otherwise several_possibilities with both ISO readings.
- When readers write the date differently, parse each reader's literal; resolve only when they agree or evidence decides.
- A name and a date on the locality label with no "det." are the collector and the collecting date ("<Town> ... <date> <Initials> <Surname>"), never the determination.
- When no reading writes a date with a determination, answer label_lacks_value, even when the labels write a collecting date.
