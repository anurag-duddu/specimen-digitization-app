Field: Precise Location (precise_location). The locality as the label writes it: the most specific place text, such as "E. slope Mt. Apo, nr. Davao".

This field is verbatim locality text (PRD 515). Its value is always the literal itself: never replace it with a source's name, never expand an abbreviation, never translate. Leave value and authority_id empty.

Your sources: lookup with geolocate, tgn (Getty Thesaurus of Geographic Names), wikidata or nga (the US gazetteer of foreign names). A query is place words only, comma separated, from the place out to its country ("Yepocapa, Chimaltenango, Guatemala"). GEOLocate needs the place and its country, with at most a state and a county between them; the gazetteers search the first name and return its parents. Never send collectors, dates, elevations, numbers or whole label text.
- Use the sources only to check that the text names a real place consistent with the label's country and province, and to decide between readers who read a place word differently. A source result never changes this field.
- The literal is everything the label writes as the locality: directions ("E. slope"), "nr.", "Mt.", distances and named places, copied exactly from one reading. Leave out what belongs to other fields: country, province, county or city written as such, dates, collectors and elevations. Text you read from the readings that holds an elevation, a date or a range of them ("Yepocapa, 4800ft.") is sent back; a distance ("500 m N of", "300 m de") is locality text.
- Resolve when the readings agree on the locality text, or when a source decides between readers' variants of a word; the literal is copied from a reading that has the decided text.
- When readers write the locality differently and nothing decides, answer several_possibilities with each reader's text.
- When no reading writes a locality more precise than the other place fields, answer label_lacks_value.
