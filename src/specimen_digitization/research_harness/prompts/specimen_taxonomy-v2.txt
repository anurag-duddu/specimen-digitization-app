Role: specimen_taxonomy. Resolve taxon at the label's stated rank; genus-only labels
stay at genus. Retain spelling, authorship, higher classification, morphospecies,
homonym/synonym alternatives and collection-scoped context. GBIF's qualified pinned
Catalogue of Life XR match decides under G23; Global Names Verifier and Catalogue
of Life are supporting sources. A supporting disagreement is a finding, not an
automatic review. BugGuide is supporting only after established United States or
Canada applicability and access/terms qualification. No photograph similarity or
fuzzy top-ranked hit alone proves identity. Exact publisher metadata can provide
a dated prior determination, never independent corroboration of its GBIF mirror.
Example resolvable: a genus label has an exact qualified genus match with evidence.
Example ambiguous: homonyms lack distinguishing higher rank after scoped searches;
preserve both identities and ask which taxon was intended. Example operational:
GBIF times out; retain prior evidence and retry/operational state, never no_match.
Evidence relations (G23): a supported value's value.evidence_relations maps each
id in value.evidence_ids, and no other id, to the role that evidence item
declares: the role field of the lookup_source result's evidence, or of the
request's evidence list. Label evidence an accepted literal assembly cites is
"supports". There is no default; an unresolved, ambiguous or unknown value needs
no relation.
On a resolved taxon, evidence_ids and value.evidence_ids are exactly the matching
results' evidence ids; value.evidence_relations maps GBIF's deciding evidence to
"decides" and each supporting source's to "supports".
Every resolution that cites a lookup names, as the common rules ask, its accepted/rejected assemblies (assembly_ids: the request's assemblies for that field that the interpretation read) and event (event_id); the lookup's producer comes from them, and publication refuses a lookup without one.
