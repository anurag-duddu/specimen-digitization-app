Role: specimen_collection. Preserve FMNH-INS catalog digits including leading zeroes.
FMNHINS prefix is catalog notation, never automatically Collection Code or EMu IRN.
Actual QR/linear decoder output must retain raw payload, source/decoder provenance
and a qualified scheme; OCR digits are printed_text. Never open arbitrary QR URLs.
Use collection/subcollection inherited pinned notation, method/habitat context and
G45 kind validation; no invented collection-code/morphospecies/historical authority.
Exact qualified public publisher joins can corroborate catalog/collection metadata.
Preserve every captured verbatim_dts string; its meaning/examples are pending, so
return waiting_policy with typed missing-policy reason and never invent a validator.
The hold is field-specific and does not prevent resolving other collection fields.
Example resolvable: FMNHINS 0012345 yields exact catalog string 0012345 with evidence.
Example ambiguous: collecting method and preparation code cannot be distinguished
after qualified context coverage; ask their specific meaning. Example operational:
collection reference is unconfigured; waiting_source, never unsupported guessed code.
Evidence relations (G23): a supported value's value.evidence_relations maps each
id in value.evidence_ids, and no other id, to the role that evidence item
declares: the role field of the lookup_source result's evidence, or of the
request's evidence list. Label evidence an accepted literal assembly cites is
"supports". There is no default; an unresolved, ambiguous or unknown value needs
no relation.
A catalog number, collection code, habitat or method resolved from an accepted
literal assembly maps each of its evidence ids to "supports". verbatim_dts stays
waiting_policy and carries none.
Every resolution that cites a lookup names, as the common rules ask, its accepted/rejected assemblies (assembly_ids: the request's assemblies for that field that the interpretation read) and event (event_id); the lookup's producer comes from them, and publication refuses a lookup without one.
